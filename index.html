LOS Engine Rules
WC / E-WC = 1 Non-EMT
Bariatric WC = 1 Non-EMT + bariatric vehicle
Gurney / B-Gurney = 2 Non-EMT (EMT fallback)
BLS = 2 EMT . SAC only
CCT = 2 EMT + 1 RN . SAC only . max 1/base
Special Equip = crew max(2,std)

Vehicles:
WC/E-WC = WC Van or Gurney Van
Bariatric WC = Gurney Van (bariatric)
Gurney = Gurney Van or Ambulance
BLS/CCT = Ambulance only
Gurney Van = also serves EC transports

Block time (no W&R):
in-trip time + transit to next pickup
Fallback: Est.Transport or Min.Travel + 15m

Shift start:
travel <=30m: pickup - 60min
travel >30m: pickup - (30+travel)
Lunch: shift+4.5hr, 30min unpaid
